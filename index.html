Welcome to the official European webshop of Hanwei. With us you can buy a real functional sword.

In 1990, Paul Chen founded the Hanwei Forge, named after the Han Dynasty, a period known for its advancements in metallurgy and weaponry. The forge was established with the vision of reviving the ancient art of sword making while incorporating modern manufacturing techniques. Located in Dalian, China, Hanwei Forge quickly gained a reputation for producing high-quality swords that were both functional and aesthetically pleasing. Hanwei Europe Shop is now a specialist in Japanese martial arts items and has an extensive assortment for the collector and serious martial arts practitioner. Hanwei has a quality wide and deep range of Japanese, European and Chinese swords.
